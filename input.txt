The Sapiens had long been accustomed to living in small bands.
Every member of the band knew one another intimately.
They shared food, helped each other in times of trouble, and argued together.
The band made collective decisions on hunting routes and migration plans.
Conflicts were usually settled through negotiation rather than violence.
However, the agricultural revolution completely changed human social structures.
Farmers began to settle in fixed villages and owned land.
Population grew rapidly, and strangers lived side by side.
New rules and beliefs were created to maintain large-scale social order.
